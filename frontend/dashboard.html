Portal Mission & Importance

Welcome to Talent Identifier, the official digital trials and selection dashboard. Conventional physical sports trials often fail to identify high-quality prospects because thousands of talented athletes in rural, district, and regional communities cannot travel due to financial and logistical barriers.

This platform overcomes these constraints by digitizing selections. Athletes upload training footage directly from their home towns. Selectors and coaches from state academies audit digital dossiers, review AI joint pose track analysis, and issue selection camp invitations instantly.
