Portal Mission & Importance

Welcome to Talent Identifier, the official digital recruitment platform bridging raw athletic potential with national selector boards. In conventional physical trials, thousands of promising athletes from rural, tribal, and district levels are overlooked due to travel constraints, lack of resources, or limited selection window hours.

This portal democratizes selections by allowing you to upload digital trial videos from anywhere. Our system automatically verifies authenticity, maps joint mechanics overlays, and compiles your biometric resume to present directly to verified selectors from state boards and sports academies.
